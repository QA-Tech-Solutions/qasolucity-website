We're looking for a QA Engineer to join our testing team and work across a range of client products. You'll be doing hands-on client work from day one, not a sandbox project, alongside a small team that actually reviews your work and gives direct feedback.

This is a hands-on testing role, not a "write test plans and hand them off" role. You'll be the person actually clicking through the product, trying to break it, and writing up exactly what happened so a developer can fix it fast.

### What a typical week looks like

- Exploratory testing on a new feature before it ships
- Writing and running structured test cases against a requirements doc
- Filing bug reports with clear steps, screenshots, and expected vs. actual behavior
- Retesting fixes and confirming nothing else broke
